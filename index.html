Simulated phishing test

This was a practice phishing email

No information was collected, and nothing has happened to your account. It takes about a minute to see what gave this one away.

1. The sender's address isn't a university address.It came from univ-mailsupport.net, not university.example.
2. It greets you as "Dear Student."Real IT notices usually use your name.
3. It threatens a deadline."Within 24 hours or incoming mail will be suspended" is meant to rush you.
4. The button goes somewhere else.Hovering showed a link to an outside site, not the university login page.

Next time, use the Report Phishing button at the top of the email. You can still report this one now and get credit for it.
